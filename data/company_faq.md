# Acme Robotics — Product FAQ (sample data for RAG demo)

## Warranty
All Acme robots ship with a 24-month limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects. Battery packs are covered for 12 months. The warranty is void if the
chassis is opened by a non-certified technician.

## Return policy
Enterprise customers may return units within 30 days for a full refund. A 15%
restocking fee applies to opened units after 14 days.

## Model lineup
- Acme Scout: indoor inspection robot, 6-hour battery, 12 kg payload.
- Acme Hauler: warehouse logistics robot, 10-hour battery, 80 kg payload.
- Acme Sentinel: outdoor security robot, IP67 rated, thermal camera.

## Support SLA
Premium support customers receive a 4-hour response SLA, 24/7. Standard support
is next-business-day. Firmware updates ship quarterly.

## Pricing
Acme Scout starts at $18,000. Acme Hauler starts at $42,000. Volume discounts
of up to 20% apply above 50 units.
